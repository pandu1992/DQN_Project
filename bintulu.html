Bintulu Port — Autonomous Navigation (BPAN)

A chart-grounded deep-Q-network case study: an autonomous vessel learns to transit the North and South Access Channels of Bintulu Port and berth at the Inner Harbours / Southern Jetty, navigating directly over the real approach chart. Runs entirely in your browser. This is a separate study from the Synthetic Port Studies 1–5.

📄 Read the BPAN paper 🎞 Animations Synthetic Port sim
Chart & scope. The background is the Bintulu Port approach chart (lateral buoys NO 1'–NO 9' / N1'–N9' on the North channel, R2'–R10' / G2'–G10' on the South channel). The navigable waypoint graph follows the charted channels and is extended into the harbour berths so the vessel completes a full sea-to-berth mission (the chart's magenta channel lines stop in open water). Geometry is a faithful approximation, not a surveyed hydrographic model.
