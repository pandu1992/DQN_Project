Browser port of the "Bintulu Port Experiment" research notebook. The DQN learns to steer the vessel forward along the planned channel to the goal while avoiding wasteful WAIT/BACKWARD moves. Toggle the heatmap to see waypoint visit frequency, export CSV for per-episode benchmark metrics, compare North vs South channel performance, and replay the best / worst episodes.

Key Findings — Experimental Studies

Three rigorous, reproducible studies: Study 1 — algorithm comparison (4 DQN variants × PER × Noisy Nets, 10 seeds); Study 2 — robustness to sensor noise & communication loss; Study 3 — confirmatory replication with a COLREGs-aware rule-based baseline. Highlights below; full reports, tables, figures, and PDF in the analysis pages.

📄 Read the unified paper & download PDF
💡 Insight

The four DQN variants are statistically indistinguishable on success and reward (no effect survives correction) — they are effectively interchangeable at this budget.

💡 Insight

Random seed dominates variance (partial η² ≈ 0.20 ≫ any design factor): run-to-run variability swamps the algorithm choice — a reproducibility caution worth reporting.

💡 Insight

The one real signal is on route optimality: Noisy Nets — not the DQN variant — is the lever with a measurable (small–medium) effect.
